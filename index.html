Privacy Policy

Drama ShortClip Finder · by LST Studio · Last updated: 18 October 2026 (ads added)

Short version: Drama ShortClip Finder has no accounts, and we (LST Studio) do not collect, sell or share your personal information. Everything you type or save stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and the only data that leaves your phone is what Google's services (the AdMob ads, and YouTube's video player and thumbnails) request when they load.

1. About the app

Drama ShortClip Finder lets you search the spoken lines of Chinese dramas, see which episode and moment a word is said, watch that moment through the YouTube player, and save a short clip of it to your phone. This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it.

2. Information we collect

We (LST Studio) do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-in, no analytics tools, and no server of our own that receives your data. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob, which may collect certain data itself; see sections 5 and 6.

3. Information stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of information in its private storage on your phone. It never leaves the device:

  • Search history – your last 20 successful searches, so you can search again quickly. You can remove one search with its × button or remove all of them with “Clear”.
  • Settings – your appearance choice (system, light or dark), your chosen clip length, and whether the speaker is muted while a clip is made.
  • Saved clips – clips you choose to download are saved in the Movies / Drama ShortClip Finder folder on your phone. You can open, share or delete them with your gallery or file manager.

The subtitles used for searching are included inside the app, so searching works without sending your search words anywhere.

4. Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy it is needed
Internet and network state To show the YouTube player, video thumbnails and ads.
Advertising ID Lets Google AdMob serve and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (see section 6).
Microphone / record audio Used only so that a downloaded clip can include the sound the app itself is playing. The app does not listen to your surroundings and does not store microphone recordings.
Screen capture (asked every time) When you tap Download, Android asks you to allow screen sharing with the app. The app records the screen while the clip plays, keeps only the video area, saves it as your clip and then deletes the temporary full-screen recording. Nothing is uploaded.
Foreground service and notifications Android requires a visible notification while a clip is being made. The notification only says that a clip is downloading.

5. Third-party services

Videos are played with the embedded YouTube player, and thumbnails are loaded from YouTube's servers. When these load, YouTube (Google) may receive technical information such as your IP address and device details and may use cookies or similar technologies, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service. We do not control that data. If you do not want this, do not use the video player or thumbnails.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad at the bottom of the main screen, provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this data, and we do not sell it. How Google handles it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice later where the tool offers it. You can also reset your advertising ID, delete it, or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some phones).

7. Content and copyright

The app does not host, store or distribute videos. Video content belongs to its respective owners and is played from YouTube. The text used for searching is an automatic transcription of public videos. You are responsible for using saved clips in line with copyright law and the rights of the content owners. If you are a rights holder and have a concern, please contact us using the address below.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children. Ads are not intended for children.

9. Data sharing and security

We do not sell, rent or share personal information. Because the app stores its own data only on your phone, it is protected by your phone's own security (screen lock and Android app sandboxing). Data that Google AdMob and YouTube collect is handled by Google as described above.

10. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Clear your search history from the home screen at any time.
  • Delete saved clips from the Movies / Drama ShortClip Finder folder.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the history and settings stored by the app.
  • You can withdraw the microphone permission in Android settings. Clips will then have no sound.
